Route Inside the Boundary First: Secure, Cost-Aware Routing for Enterprise LLM Inference

Pujitha Sri Lakshmi Paladugu
Independent Researcher · ORCID 0009-0001-5678-4022

Preprint · 2026-10-05 · 9 pages · Full text (PDF)

Abstract

Enterprises that adopt large language models face two coupled constraints: inference cost and data governance. Model-routing research cuts cost by sending easy requests to cheap models, but it treats where a prompt's data goes as incidental. We argue that the data boundary should be the first routing decision, not a filter added later. We present a routing architecture that classifies sensitivity before any cost optimization, keeps restricted prompts on self-hosted models by construction, escalates only non-sensitive hard requests to frontier models, and meters data egress as a first-class metric. A simple analytical model shows that savings depend at least as much on the escalation rate as on model price, that escalation also drives data egress, and that silent quality regression, not visible failure, is the risk to monitor. We close with three practices teams can adopt now.

Keywords: large language models; model routing; inference cost; data governance; zero trust; AI engineering
